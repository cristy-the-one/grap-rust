# Romanian legal knowledge

GRAP discovers and connects knowledge stated in collections of Romanian legal
documents while preserving the sources and the limits of each assertion.

## Language

**Source document**:
A particular downloaded or supplied representation of legal text and its metadata.
Several source documents can describe the same legal act or different versions of it.

**Legal act**:
An identified law, ordinance, decision, order or other legal instrument, distinct
from the document that describes it and from other acts it cites.

**Provision**:
A scoped part of a legal act, such as an article, paragraph, item or annex.
An article number alone does not identify a provision across acts.

**Claim**:
An assertion extracted from a particular source, with its supporting passage.
A claim is a proposal about the source's meaning, not a guarantee of legal truth.
_Avoid_: Verified fact, ground truth

**Obligation**:
A duty imposed on a specified subject, including its conditions, exceptions,
limits and relevant dates. It is distinct from a description or a permission.

**Amendment**:
A stated change to a particular act or provision. Its scope and date are part of
the claim; a citation alone does not establish an amendment.

**Act version**:
The text or legal state of an act at a specified point in its history.
Downloading a document recently does not establish that its text is current law.

**Alias**:
Another name for the same entity in a specified scope. Similar wording or topic
does not establish identity, and a locally defined abbreviation need not be global.

**Coverage**:
The identified sources, acts and provisions represented by the system, together
with known missing, unprocessed or unresolved material. A large graph alone does
not establish comprehensive coverage.
